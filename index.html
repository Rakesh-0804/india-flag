Jana-gana-mana-adhinayaka jaya he

Thou art the ruler of the minds of all people, dispenser of India's destiny.

Flag Code of India Guidelines 🇮🇳

1. Display & Position of Honor

The Flag must always be flown with the Saffron band on top. When displayed horizontally or at an angle, Saffron should be at the top. When placed on a stage or podium, it must be to the right of the speaker.

2. Flag Code 2002 Amendment

As per the amended Flag Code of India 2002, member of the general public, private organizations, and educational institutions are allowed to hoist the National Flag on all days, day and night, with dignity and respect.

3. Dignified Disposal Protocol

When the Flag is damaged or discolored, it must not be discarded casually. It should be destroyed as a whole in private, preferably by burning or burial, in a manner consistent with its dignity.
