Heritage, History & Trivia

Discover the rich history behind the creation of the Indian National Flag and challenge your knowledge with our interactive quiz.

Designed by Pingali Venkayya

Freedom fighter and agriculturist Pingali Venkayya designed the original flag in Vijayawada. Originally featuring red and green bands, Mahatma Gandhi later suggested adding a white band and the spinning wheel (Charkha) in the center.

Adopted on 22 July 1947

The Constituent Assembly adopted the tricolor in its present form on July 22, 1947, just 24 days before India gained Independence. The spinning wheel was replaced by the Ashoka Chakra from the Lion Capital of Ashoka at Sarnath.

Official Flag Code Proportions

The ratio of the length of the flag to its height is strictly 3:2. All three horizontal bands must be of equal size, with the Ashoka Chakra centered in the white band featuring 24 evenly spaced spokes.
